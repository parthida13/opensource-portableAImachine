Required Parts:


NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Developer Kit
5000mAh lithium-ion rechargeable battery
Usb C cables to charge the lithium battery and for the headless setup
NVMe SSD (ideally 128- 256 GB) for read/write speed




What am I going to make?

I am looking forward to creating a small but powerful AI machine/server that is small and sleek and power efficient

Why do people need this?

Nowadays, large AI data centers process data and take up a lot of resources for cooling and maintaining efficient token speed. According to the privacy policies of companies like Anthropic and OpenAI, it is written that user data is not private[¹][²]. Whatever you ask these AI models, it gets processed on their servers and stored there for moderation training, etc. This means that if you provide any personal or sensitive information like your name, date of birth, face, and even the names of people you know, that means your life is not private; it's in the hands of these companies.




How is this project/piece of hardware any different?

The catch with this project is that you are the one who owns your data. You get to be able to use your own personal AI that remembers you, can recognize you, and is overall your personal companion, similarly to Jarvis, the AI model used by genius billionaire philanthropist Tony Stark in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

What benefits can I encounter?

You will get many benefits from this hardware, and you will also understand why this hardware is worth investing in.

It lives on a personal ‘cloud’: You own all the information, and it stays with you

It understands you: You get to train your AI model to your needs. You can make it think as you act like you and get things done like you

No need for the internet: No wifi? No problem. This hardware works offline too; you won’t need to fret whenever you don't have a signal.

Lithium Battery: This hardware project is expected to use a Lithium-Ion battery with 5000mAh. Similar to the ones found in your smartphone/powerbanks. For safety reasons I might use a 5000mAh powerbank instead

Repurposed use: This hardware can also function as basically anything else. If you don’t like the idea of an AI server in your hand, you can repurpose it into, say, a Plex media server, a Minecraft server, a storage system in the cloud (with a good SSD) – I mean the possibilities are endless.




Problems for performance:


Might not be as powerful as top models (Fable, Opus, GPT5.6Sol): Since most LLM’s aren’t that powerful compared to top models like Fable, Opus, or even GPT5.6Sol. But still, you get to train your AI, and you get to code your own LLM/Neural network. You can import any open-source AI

Power usage: Although we are hoping for performance, this 5000mAh battery might not cut it if you use it 24/7. With cooling and other components, this is basically a mini supercomputer which might last a few hours on full charge. I am trying to find solutions, one of which is creating a script that sleeps the system or shuts it down until no device is connected to the hotspot or if no response is given.
